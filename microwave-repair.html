Common Problems

Microwave Oven Repair problems we fix

Not Heating

Faulty magnetron, diode or capacitor stopping the oven from heating.

Sparking Inside

Damaged waveguide cover or food residue causing sparks.

Turntable Not Rotating

Worn motor or coupler stopping the plate from moving.

Door Not Closing

Broken latch or hinge preventing safe operation.

Panel / Display Faults

Touch panel and control board repair.

Tripping Power

Short circuits and fuse faults diagnosed safely.

FAQ

Microwave Oven Repair repair questions

Why is my microwave running but not heating?

Often a faulty magnetron, high-voltage diode or capacitor. Please do not open it yourself - the parts hold dangerous charge.

Is it safe to use a microwave that sparks?

No. Stop using it and call us for inspection.

Do you repair convection and solo microwaves?

Yes, we repair solo, grill and convection microwave ovens.

How long does a repair take?

Many microwave repairs are completed in a single visit, depending on parts availability.
